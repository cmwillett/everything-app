How Everything App works

Tap a topic to open it. Everything you add saves by itself and shows up on your other devices within a second or two.

🏠 Home

Your overview. The boxes at the top count your tasks, reminders, links and notes — tap one to jump there.

Daily checklist is a short routine that un-checks itself every morning. Tap Edit to change the items.

Below that you can see what's on for Today (and Later / Archived), and your latest notes and lists.

✅ Tasks

Type a task name, add details if you like (bold, lists and links work), and tap Add task.

  • ☆ / ★ moves a task into Today or back to Later.
  • The status menu marks it To Do, In Progress, Blocked or Done.
  • ⇩ archives a finished task (out of the way, not deleted); ⇧ brings it back.
  • ✎ edits, ✕ deletes, and dragging the ⠿ handle reorders.
  • The chips above the list (All, To Do…) filter by status.
🔔 Reminders

Write what to remember, pick a date and time, and tap Add reminder. At that time a notification pops up on every phone where you've turned notifications on — even if the app is closed.

  • You can create reminders on any device (like your computer); they still arrive on your phone.
  • Sent reminders move to Sent. Tap ↻ to set one again.
  • Notifications are turned on per device. Use the bar on this tab, or the round account menu → Turn on notifications here. Don't want them on this device? Tap Not on this device.
  • iPhone: notifications only work in the Home Screen app (see “Install it on your phone” below).
  • Account menu → Send a test notification checks everything's working.
🔗 Links

Save websites you use a lot. Give it a label, the address, and a category (e.g. School, Bills) — links are grouped by category. Tap a link to open it.

📝 Notes & lists

＋ New note makes a sticky note. Tap it to type — a formatting bar (bold, italic, bullets, links…) appears while you're editing.

☑ New list makes a checklist. Type in “Add an item” and press Enter to keep adding. Tap a box to check something off — it's crossed out and moves to the bottom. Clear checked removes finished items.

The coloured dots change a card's colour, ⠿ drags it, ✕ deletes it.

🎨 Make it yours

Tap the round button with your initial (top right) → Appearance. Pick a theme (Blush, Lavender, Peach…), light or dark, an accent colour, a background (hearts!) and a rounded font. It's saved to your account, so it follows you to your other devices and doesn't change anyone else's.

📱 Install it on your phone
  • iPhone: open the site in Safari → Share button → Add to Home Screen. Then open it from the Home Screen and sign in there.
  • Android: Chrome menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen / Install app.
🔒 Accounts, privacy & backups

Everyone signs in with their own account and only ever sees their own things. It works offline too — changes catch up when you're back online.

Account menu → Export backup saves a copy of everything to a file; Import backup restores one (it replaces what's there). Sign out also stops this device getting your reminders.

📊 Admin dashboard

Counts only — nobody's actual tasks, notes or reminders are shown here.
